Price changes, on one scale

Each item starts at 100 in September 2021. The lines show how its price changed since.

How to read this: The cards show BLS U.S. city average prices for specific item definitions. The comparison chart resets each item to 100 in September 2021 so different units can be compared. It is not a cost-of-living index or a meal price.

BLS average prices come from prices collected for the CPI. Items have fixed definitions and units; coverage and publication can vary, and missing months are left blank. These are nominal prices, not adjusted for inflation elsewhere in the economy.

Sources: BLS: Average Price Data · BLS: published item definitions · FRED series data

SMALL PRINT

Breakfast Index is an independent research experiment. Its lead chart compares published average hourly earnings with restaurant-food consumer prices, normalized to January 2025. It does not measure actual breakfast purchases, household disposable income, or individual budgets. Consumer surveys and the separate morning-traffic pilot have different methods and are not combined into the lead measure. Source releases may be delayed or revised. Not an official economic measure, forecast, or financial advice; no restaurant affiliation.
