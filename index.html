HOW WE CALCULATE. A small basket. The same measurements. Over time. We compare a consistent breakfast price with hourly take-home earnings to estimate minutes worked per breakfast. Food, fuel, household costs and confidence are tracked as separate dated series. No synthetic historical points and no unvalidated composite score. The trend matters more than any single reading.

SMALL PRINT

Breakfast Index is an independent experiment based on a limited sample of locations, not a nationally representative measure. It is not an official economic measure, an economic forecast, or financial advice. Readings may be incomplete or revised. Sentiment and confidence surveys are not direct affordability measures; their scales are not comparable. No affiliation with any restaurant or brand.
